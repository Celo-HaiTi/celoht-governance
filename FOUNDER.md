# Founder

## Johnny Dubic — Founder of CeloHT

Johnny Dubic is permanently recognized as the Founder of CeloHT. This is a
founding, historical, and institutional designation. It does not by itself
create an executive appointment or governance privilege.

Founder status does not automatically make Johnny Dubic CEO, Director,
Executive Director, Managing Director, Manager, Foundation Director, a
Governance Council member, or a repository maintainer. No such appointment is
established by this document.

The Founder has no automatic Council seat or vote, Founder safeguard, veto,
emergency override, or unilateral governance authority. The Founder may
provide historical context, submit proposals, and participate in deliberation,
but must follow the same documented governance process as every other
participant. A proposal submitted by the Founder is not an official CeloHT
decision until it completes the collective process in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md).

FreClean is separate from CeloHT governance. Nothing in this document creates
a CeloHT department, Council, foundation, legal entity, or relationship with
FreClean.
