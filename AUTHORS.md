# Authors and Contributors

CeloHT recognizes **Johnny Dubic — Permanently Recognized Founder of CeloHT**
as a founding and historical designation.

Permanent founder recognition is historical and institutional; it does not
confer perpetual governance authority, ownership rights, veto power, or
unilateral control.

Founder status is not an executive appointment, repository ownership grant,
maintainer assignment, Council seat, Council vote, veto, emergency override,
or unilateral governance authority. Authorship, contribution, and governance
authority are separate concerns. Contributions and decisions must follow the
documented process in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md).

This file does not claim a complete contributor roster or invent additional
roles. Verification of the Founder role remains **Pending Verification** under
[`VERIFICATION_POLICY.md`](./VERIFICATION_POLICY.md).
