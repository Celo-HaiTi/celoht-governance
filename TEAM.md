# Celo-HaiTi Team

## Founder

**Name:** Johnny Dubic

**Role:** Permanently Recognized Founder of Celo-HaiTi

The Founder designation recognizes founding history, mission origins, and
institutional continuity. Permanent founder recognition is historical and
institutional; it does not confer perpetual governance authority, ownership
rights, veto power, or unilateral control. It does not automatically establish a CEO, Director,
Executive Director, Managing Director, Manager, Foundation Director,
maintainer, repository owner, Governance Council seat, or Governance Council
vote.

The Founder has no safeguard, veto, emergency override, or unilateral
governance authority. The Founder may contribute historical and technical
context, submit proposals, and participate in community deliberation under the
same rules as other participants. See [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md).

No additional team members, maintainers, or Council members are established by
this document. Appointments must be documented through the applicable Celo-HaiTi
process rather than inferred from founder status.
