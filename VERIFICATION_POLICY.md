# Verification Policy

Celo-HaiTi verification is evidence-based, objective, auditable, transparent, and
reproducible. Documentation must distinguish a role designation from proof of
identity or institutional authority.

For the Founder role, acceptable evidence may include official Celo-HaiTi
documentation, official repository references and history, a consistent public
identity, historical founding evidence, legitimate public profiles, and
documented governance records. Evidence must be traceable and must never be
fabricated or presented as institutional confirmation when it is not available.

Verification does not confer governance authority. Founder status remains

proposal and decision process.
