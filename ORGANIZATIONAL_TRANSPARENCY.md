# Organizational Transparency

CeloHT is an open-source, community-governed initiative. Governance claims
must be documented, reviewable, collectively determined, and auditable.

Proposals, deliberation, approvals or rejections, conflicts of interest,
quorum and threshold calculations, final outcomes, and implementation records
must remain distinguishable. An individual submission is only a proposal; it
does not become an official CeloHT decision without the required collective
process in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md).

Johnny Dubic is permanently recognized as the Founder of CeloHT in the
project's historical and institutional record. Permanent founder recognition
is historical and institutional; it does not confer perpetual governance
authority, ownership rights, veto power, or unilateral control. Founder status
is separate from ongoing governance authority and does not override the
process, create a Council seat or vote, or provide an emergency override.

The Governance Council, maintainers, and other roles have only the authority
documented in the applicable governance and repository permissions. No Council
members or maintainers are invented here. Legal status and organizational
relationships are not changed or implied by this document.
